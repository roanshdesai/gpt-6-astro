# Demo video script (30 seconds)

The rules allow a video of **30 seconds or less** (or 7 slides). Over the
limit is ineligible, so aim for 28 seconds. Roansh records and cuts; this is
the script and shot list. About 70 spoken words fit in 28 seconds.

Every line below is backed by the site's data. Do not add numbers or claims
that are not on the site.

| Time | On screen | Voice-over or caption |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:05 | Landing. The flame on **Earth**: tall orange teardrop. Flip the gravity switch: it relaxes into a dim blue sphere. | "In space, fire has no up." |
| 0:05–0:09 | Scroll past the three incident cards (Apollo 1, Apollo 13, Mir). | "Fire has threatened crews before. NASA has run 24 experiments to understand it." |
| 0:09–0:13 | The board: three columns, Prevent, Detect, Suppress. Pointer hovers a card. | "We turned them into answers to the questions that matter." |
| 0:13–0:21 | Open **"Does switching off the fans stop it?"** Answer appears, evidence rows arrive one by one, strength meter ticks to **4 of 24, 4 with stated results**. Hover a citation chip to show the NASA quote. | "Not reliably. Every answer is ranked by evidence and shows NASA's exact words: what was tested, expected, or found." |
| 0:21–0:25 | Scroll to **Not yet known**, then open the **Moon and Mars** question; show the line that none of the 24 tested Moon or Mars gravity. | "And it is honest about what is still unknown." |
| 0:25–0:28 | Investigation page or Compare, then the site URL and project name. | "The Question Board. NASA data, every quote checked." |

## Notes for recording

- Record at 1920×1080, 60 fps if the flame stays smooth; cut to 30 fps if the
  file is too large.
- Use the deployed site, not localhost, so the URL bar (if shown) is the real
  one.
- The fans-off answer is the strongest clip: all 4 of its investigations
  have stated results, and its answer is a clear "Not reliably". Check the
  number on screen matches the site on the day.
- Captions: burn them in. Judges may watch muted.
- If any shot uses SI-generated images or video, it needs a visible
  watermark (challenge rule). Screen recordings of the site do not.
- End card: project name and the live URL, on screen for at least 2 seconds.

## Fallback: 7 slides

If the video is not ready at feature freeze (Sun 6:00 PM IST):

1. Title, one line, screenshot of the flame on Earth and in freefall.
2. The problem: 24 NASA investigations, results hard to find and compare.
3. The board: screenshot of the three columns.
4. One answer: fans-off, with the strength meter and a quote.
5. Not yet known: the Moon and Mars answer.
6. How we keep it true: tested / expected / found labels, word-for-word check that fails the build.
7. Links: live site, repo, NASA PSI.
