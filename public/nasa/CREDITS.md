## Changes made to the originals

- candle-earth.jpg, candle-microgravity.jpg: cropped from the 2500 x 2000 original of GRC-1998-C-00486 to remove the white border, NASA insignia and caption text, and to split the two panels (re-saved as JPEG by sips; no retouching).
- bass-blue-flame.jpg: downscaled from 4288 x 2848 to 2400 px wide, JPEG quality 82.
- apollo-13-service-module.jpg: downscaled from 3000 x 1878 to 2200 px wide, JPEG quality 80 (to get under 700 KB).
- acme-flame-design-soot-sphere.jpg: converted from a 1.5 MB PNG to JPEG (quality 90) and 12 px cropped off the bottom to remove a stray white line.
- same-msg-walker.jpg: 79 px cropped off the bottom to remove the white strip carrying the photo ID.
- All other files are byte-for-byte as downloaded: the library's "large" rendition (or the original where no large exists), or the nasa.gov file at the URL listed.

## Things this folder does not contain

- No photograph of the 1997 Mir fire. None was found in the searches run; do not caption any image here as the fire.
- No exterior or hatch photograph of the burned Apollo 1 spacecraft. The searches run turned up only the interior views (S67-21294, S67-21295) and a 2017 museum photo of the hatch (KSC-20170124-PH_KLS01_0129, not downloaded).
- No full-resolution natural-colour FLEX droplet flame. The only large FLEX image on nasa.gov (551838main_flex_full.jpg) is a colourised composite of backlit video frames and was left out.
- No s-Flame or CFI-G image specifically. The two ACME images are from Flame Design, which uses the same spherical burner.
- No flame or smoke imagery from SAME; the searches run found only crew-at-hardware photos.
- GRC-2002-C-00464 is titled "Candle flame in microgravity 2" in the library but actually shows a circuit board; it was rejected.
